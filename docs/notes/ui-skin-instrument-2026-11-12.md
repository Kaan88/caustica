## UI skin: Instrument (2026-11-12)
The app's own look in place of egui's stock one. The specification is the board `docs/mockups/skin-b-instrument.dc.html` (its `<style>` block); the code is `src/ui/skin.rs`. Step 2 of the pass is built: the Render tab. The other tabs, the bars and the windows still have the old look; the plan and the open items are in the roadmap (Plan step 1).

- **Technique (decided by trying it on a button):** egui's painter has no gradient fill and no inner shadow, so a control's chrome is drawn on the CPU as a CSS box (`raster`: rounded border box, one-point border, vertical two-stop fill, `brightness()`, inset and outer `box-shadow`s with offset, blur and spread) at the screen's pixels per point, and painted as a textured mesh (`chrome`). Edges come from a signed distance to the rounded box; a blurred shadow is a Gaussian of standard deviation blur / 2 on that distance, which is exact along straight edges and close in the corners. The mesh route (per-vertex colours) was dropped: egui feathers only its own shapes, so gradient corners would need hand-made anti-aliasing, and an inner shadow would still need a texture.
- **One atlas** (`Atlas`): both renderers keep each egui texture as its own resource with a small slot table and re-upload it whole, so the looks share one texture (512 pixels square, doubling when full). A look is drawn the first time it is needed and `skin::flush` (end of the UI pass, `App::redraw`) uploads the atlas once in that frame. A full atlas is kept for the frame that filled it (its meshes point into it) and the next frame starts over one size up, to 4096 at most. It starts over when the pixels per point change (Ctrl + / −), so the chrome is sharp at every zoom.
- **Slicing** (`Stretch`): a button's gradient fixes its height, so buttons, fields and the groove are keyed by height and stretch in width only (three slices; the middle is one texel column). Popups and focus rings have flat fills and stretch both ways (nine slices). Thumb, checkbox, switch track and knob are drawn at their size. Rects are snapped to whole pixels so texels land on pixels.
- **Looks** (`Look`, `spec`): raised (rest, hover, pressed), on (the pressed-in toggle, with its hover and pressed brightness), flat hover, primary, field (rest, hover, focus), groove, thumb, checkbox, switch track and knob, popup, focus rings, the segment's shared drop shadow. `Seg` gives a segment's pieces their corners; neighbours overlap by one point so they share a border.
- **States and motion:** a control paints its rest look and fades the hover and pressed looks over it (100 ms and 60 ms, `animate_bool_with_time`), so nothing repaints at rest. The switch knob and the section arrow use the board's curve, cubic-bezier(.3, .7, .3, 1), over 140 ms (`ease`). Disabled is egui's own fade with `disabled_alpha` 0.42 in the scope. Focus is an accent ring (1.5 points; the primary button's has a gap in the panel colour; checkbox and switch get an outline 1.5 points off the control, `Spec::hole`).
- **Widgets:** `button`, `primary`, `flat`, `toggle`, `segment`, `combo` with `menu`, `item`, `item_value` and `separator`, `checkbox`, `checkbox_label`, `switch`, `slider`, `field`, `fold` (the section band), `title`. They allocate and paint themselves. Two reuse egui for the typing: `field` paints the recess behind a stock text widget whose own chrome is switched off (`bare`), and the slider's number is an `egui::DragValue` in such a field.
- **Slider:** the app's `Slider` (main.rs) now keeps its settings (`skin::SliderArgs`) and builds egui's slider outside the scope or `skin::slider` inside it. The rail is the skin's own (drag with smart aim; Left and Right step one point of travel and at least to the next value the rounding keeps, as egui's `stepped_value`; a typed number is rounded like a dragged one); the value mapping is egui 0.37's `slider_core` functions, copied because egui keeps them private, so a value sits where egui's slider puts it and logarithmic sliders behave the same. It is laid out from the right: the number (58 points, wider when its text needs it, right-aligned in Plex Mono), then the rail in what is left. The thumb's centre runs the whole groove, as on the board, so it overhangs by 5 points at either end.
- **The "modified" dot** takes no room in the skin: it sits before the rail, in a 5-point slot that ends where the thumb's overhang begins (so a thumb at its minimum can still be grabbed), and number fields end where combos do. Outside the skin it keeps its own 8 points after the number.
- **Scope (2026-11-13: the skin is app-wide):** `ui::theme` installs `skin::style` and the fonts, and every call site uses the skin's widgets (`Button`, `ComboBox`, `button`, `selectable`, `checkbox_label`, …; inside a menu they draw as menu rows). `skin::scope(ui, bleed, …)` now only marks the properties panel (`skin::fills()`): there a row's combo and slider fill the controls column and the section bands bleed across the panel's 12-point padding. Elsewhere a combo is as wide as its text and a slider has the stock length. The shared helpers (`row`, `check_row`, `grid` with its 128-point label column and 28-point rows, `fold`, `tab_title`, `note`, `section`, `drag_group`) draw the skin everywhere. The first, Render-tab-only stage with a `skin::on()` switch and the old look beside it is gone.
- **Fonts:** IBM Plex Sans (regular, semibold) and IBM Plex Mono are built in (`assets/fonts`, OFL 1.1, listed in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES and copied into `licenses/` by both packagers) as three named families (`skin::sans`, `semibold`, `mono`) that fall back to egui's own fonts and Hack. The stock Proportional and Monospace families are unchanged, so the unskinned UI keeps its metrics. `ui_strings_have_glyphs` checks the new families too. Sizes: 12.5 text, 14 semibold title, 11 semibold caps with 0.66 points of tracking for section names, 11.5 mono for numbers, 11 mono for header previews.
- **HDR:** `gui.hlsl` blends the UI on sRGB code values in SDR, as CSS does, but in linear light on an scRGB swap chain. There a 5 % white line came out about three times too strong and a drop shadow at half its weight (measured in a capture: 73 instead of 45, 29 instead of 25 on the panel's 34). `for_linear_blend` changes the alpha of every translucent pixel so that linear blending over the panel colour gives the code-value result; `App::ui` tells the skin the mode each frame (`blend_in_linear_light`) and the atlas is redrawn when it changes. Over other backdrops (a popup's shadow over the image) it is approximate. HDR10 blends on PQ code values and is left as it is.
- **Text weight in HDR (2026-11-12):** egui's dark-mode glyph curve (alpha = 2c − c²) thickens edges for a blend on code values. On scRGB the blend is in linear light, which thickens light text on a dark panel by about as much again, so every font came out a weight bolder: the old thin font carried it, Plex Regular at 12.5 points turned smudgy (the owner's "text rendering is worse"; the SDR captures never showed it). `App::ui` now sets the curve to plain coverage (`Gamma(1)`) while the UI is blended in linear light, which lands within 4 % of the SDR result for the UI's text colours. It is egui-wide, so the unskinned tabs' text is also thinner in HDR than it was: the same as in SDR. Tried and measured without gain: hinting targets, the auto-hinter (Plex's own instructions fit vertically only), sub-pixel binning off, whole-pixel sizes.
- **Idle repaints (2026-11-12):** a converged render draws only on input, so a combo opened when the pointer next moved (a popup needs a second frame) and fades froze. `App::redraw` now requests the next frame when egui's root viewport asks for one with no delay.
- **Control sheet:** `--ui-skin-sheet` draws every control in rest, hover, pressed, focused and disabled (states forced through `FORCED`), a menu, a segment, a combo held open and the type scale, as the board's sheet does. A headless capture has no pointer, so this is how the states are checked against the board.
- **Render tab:** all rows use the skin. The combos fill the controls column. Light sampling is a segment. Every on/off row is a checkbox (owner, 2026-11-12: no switches in rows; `skin::switch` stays for the sheet and for places outside rows). "CPU truth" is a nested section band.
- **The rest of the app (2026-11-13):** top bar (36 points, gradient, joined icon pairs, the sample counter as a recessed readout), tool rail, tab strip, outliner (band header, flat rows), status bar (24 points, #1a1a1c, accent progress), timeline, transform strip, browsers (cards with a recessed picture well), windows (`skin::window` with `show_skin`), menus, tooltips, the palette and toasts (`skin::card`). A colour is edited in the skin's own picker (`color_rgb`: saturation/value square, hue strip, R G B fields); it writes back only on an edit, because the HSV round trip is not bit-exact and would restart the render. `field` gives a number with a long unit the width it came to last frame, while the same width is asked for. A heading inside a grid must be `skin::heading` in a cell: `section` adds space, which panics in a grid (it crashed the Lights tab with the physical sky on).
- **Deviations from the board, for the owner to judge:**
  - Pressed content moves a whole pixel at 100 % scale, not half a point: egui snaps text to pixels, and unsnapped text would blur.
  - A slider's unit ("spp", "px") is not in the number field (owner, 2026-11-12), so every field is 58 points and the rails are one length. `Slider` (main.rs) draws it in faint Plex Mono 11 at the end of the label column, left of the "modified" dot, when the slider is the row's first control and the label leaves room (`ui::row_unit_room`, from the label's and controls' edges that `row_controls` records). Otherwise it stays in the field: "(BDPT stops at 24)", and "× max" after the fireflies checkbox. Samples per frame lost its "spp" (the label says it).
  - Tooltips and the tab's surroundings (outliner header, tab strip, bars) are still the old look until step 3.
- **Shared parts** (`skin.rs`): `text_edit`, `text_area` and `drag_value` put egui's text widgets in the recessed field (every `TextEdit` and `DragValue` goes through them); `radio` and `radio_value` are the checkbox, round. Menus: `menu_button` (flat top-level, or a submenu row), `menu_button_raised`, `menu_button_sticky`, `menu_on` (a menu from a button the caller drew), `context_menu`, `color_rgb`. A menu's popup runs with `menu_look`, which makes egui's own frame invisible and keeps its 4 points of padding; `menu_content` paints `Look::Popup` behind the rows. `card` is the same chrome for floating UI that is not a menu (toasts, the palette). `window(title)` with `show_skin(ctx, ..)` is a floating window (`window_frame`, plus the top highlight an egui frame cannot draw); the Scenes and Assets browsers fill the screen and use `panel_frame`. `button_chrome` gives a bar's icon button the push-button looks. `fold_with` keeps a section's state under its own salt; `disclosure` is the "More" row. The global style (`skin::style`) is the board's `.tip`, so tooltips, and any popup nobody framed, get it; menus, combos, windows and cards never read it. Tooltip text is the body size (12.5), not the board's 11.5: egui lays a tooltip out from the global body style.
- **Tests** (`skin::tests`): a raised button pixel by pixel (edge, highlight, gradient ends, corner, drop shadow), the pressed shading, the same look at 150 %, the linear-light correction against gui.hlsl's formula, the eased curve, the copied slider scale, the outline's gap, the atlas packing, reset and regrow.
- **Late additions:** `chevron_toggle` (a feature's arrow), `group_row` and `band_title` (the outliner's group rows and header), `selectable_mono` (list rows of fixed-pitch columns, the path list), `segment_tipped` (a segment with a hover text per button), `menu_heading`. The picker behind `color_rgb` is `skin::picker`: a saturation/value square and hue strip painted as meshes, R, G, B as `drag_value`s; it is on the sheet. A window's collapse arrow is egui's triangle shape; `show_skin` swaps it for the chevron after the window is drawn (`restyle_collapse_arrow`). `field` keeps the width its content came to last frame, so a number with a unit ("0.0 units") makes room instead of hanging over its slider. Outliner rows are flat buttons (`button_chrome`), pressed in with accent text while selected, and the list is cut at a whole row. The control sheet `--ui-skin-sheet` also shows the picker. `--ui-describe` opens the AI describe window for captures; the timeline shows in a capture of any keyed scene (`--scene scenes/builtin/sunlit-room-day-cycle.json`).
