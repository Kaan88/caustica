# Scene mesh credits

**Utah teapot:** Martin Newell's original Bézier patch model, from
[three.js r180 TeapotGeometry](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r180/examples/jsm/geometries/TeapotGeometry.js).
The source and the three.js MIT license are included here. `tools/build_teapot_data.py`
extracts the control points; the Rust engine tessellates the patches itself.

**Stanford bunny:** Stanford University Computer Graphics Laboratory, scanned by
Greg Turk and Marc Levoy. This is the 35,947-vertex / 69,451-triangle zippered reconstruction,
obtained from [Alec Jacobson's common test-model mirror](https://github.com/alecjacobson/common-3d-test-models/blob/master/data/stanford-bunny.obj)
when Stanford's archive download was unavailable. The engine computes smooth normals.

[Stanford's original repository and usage terms](https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/)
permit research use and free redistribution with credit; commercial use needs their
permission. The bunny asset and rendered bunny examples retain those terms. Other
procedural shapes and scene arrangements are generated by this project.
